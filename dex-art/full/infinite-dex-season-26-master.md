# Infinite Dex — Season 26 Master Roster

## System

- Season reward set: 50 cards, numbered `01/50` through `50/50`.
- Infinite Dex: 25 original creatures, numbered `#001` through `#025`.
- Each creature may appear on two different reward cards/forms while retaining the same Dex number.
- Every card displays `infinitepulls.com` for attribution when shared.
- Required card fields: `INFINITE DEX`, `SEASON 26`, creature name and Dex number, reward name, reward-set number, and `infinitepulls.com`.
- Art size: true 5:7 portrait card; production target 1060 × 1484 px.

## Secret completion card

- `51/50` is intentionally numbered beyond the official 50-card set.
- Unlock condition: the collector has earned every standard card from `01/50` through `50/50`.
- The secret card uses a black-and-gold visual treatment distinct from the standard prismatic palette.
- Glimbit retains Dex number `#001`; this is a third, completion-only form rather than a new creature.

## Visual rules established by batch 01–10

- Premium layered metallic frame with holographic crystal accents.
- Creature is the large central hero; reward name is the largest text.
- Header stays consistent while dominant palette changes by creature.
- Identity plaque format: `CREATURE · DEX #000`.
- Footer format: `00/50 · infinitepulls.com`.
- No address, QR code, stats, powers, achievement instructions, or filler text on the card face.
- All creatures and worlds are original Infinite Pulls intellectual property; avoid recognizable third-party characters and trade dress.
