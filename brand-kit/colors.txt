THE THREE THAT DO THE WORK

Black #000000     the background of nearly everything
Blue #007FFF      the lightning and the glow behind the content
Gold #FFC928      the headline, the panel borders, the bolts

GOLD HAS THREE SHADES, and a headline needs all three to look bevelled
Gold highlight: #FFD400
Gold mid: #FFC928
Gold shadow: #BF7F00

BLUE HAS THREE TOO, brightest at the strike and darkest at the edges
Blue bright: #3F9FFF
Blue mid: #007FFF
Blue deep: #0054FF

WHAT THE COLOURS MEAN — do not mix these up
Green #008000     a price went UP. Only ever this.
Red #E1252B       a price went DOWN. Only ever this.
White #FFFFFF     the square behind the QR code, and chrome highlights
