The Collection After a Loss

@FreshPackFrank asks

a loved one left me cards and I can't decide whether to keep or sell them. Where would you start?

Jeff answers

I'd begin by separating the cards with personal meaning from decisions that can wait. Photograph the collection and preserve any notes. If value becomes part of the conversation, use our app for initial references and get help identifying the important pieces. You don't have to make every decision while everything still feels raw.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
