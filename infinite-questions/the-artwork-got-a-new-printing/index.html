The Artwork Got a New Printing

@NervousNinetales asks

if an artwork gets reused, does the original lose its reason to be valuable?

Jeff answers

Not necessarily, but I wouldn't assume no effect either. Collectors may care about the original release, the artwork itself, or both. I'd compare the exact versions through our app and matching sales over time. Different buyers can respond differently, so the new printing needs context rather than a blanket rule.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
