The Storage Check I Never Do

@FreshPackFrank asks

once cards are stored safely, do you ever need to check on them?

Jeff answers

I'd occasionally inspect the storage area for moisture, crowding, damaged containers, and anything pressing where it shouldn't. You don't need to unsleeve every card. A quick check when reorganizing can catch a changed environment before it becomes a collection-wide surprise.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
