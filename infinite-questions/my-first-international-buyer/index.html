My First International Buyer

@SpreadsheetSnorlax asks

someone overseas wants my card. What should I understand before saying yes?

Jeff answers

I'd check the selling platform and carrier requirements, available tracking, packaging rules, documentation, and the full shipping cost. Be clear about responsibilities for any charges. Don't promise a delivery date you can't control. Starting with familiar destinations is also fine while you learn the process.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
