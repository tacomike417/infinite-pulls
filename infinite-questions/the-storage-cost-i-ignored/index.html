The Storage Cost I Ignored

@AtticRattata asks

I'm paying for extra storage mostly because of sealed stuff. Should that count against what I make later?

Jeff answers

I'd include meaningful holding expenses when deciding whether the activity makes sense for you. Keep records rather than relying on the final sale price alone. Our app helps with supported card references, but it doesn't erase real costs elsewhere. Sometimes reducing the collection simplifies more than the shelves.
