An Artist I Keep Noticing

@PaydayPsyduck asks

I keep picking cards by the same artist without realizing it. Can I collect just their stuff?

Jeff answers

Absolutely. Remember @NewKidInKanton collecting everyday Pokémon scenes? Following an artist can give you that same direction. Start a checklist of work you actually like, rather than feeling obligated to own everything they've illustrated. A recognizable thread makes a small binder feel personal.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
