The Card with a Note

@ClosetCleanoutCrew asks

is it cheesy to give someone an inexpensive card with a note about why it reminds me of them?

Jeff answers

I think that's a thoughtful gift. Keep the note separate from the card so both can be preserved safely. @JustHereForPikachu valued the story behind a first pull more than the market number. Giving somebody another story for their binder is a perfectly good present.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
