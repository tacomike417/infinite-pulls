The Friend Who Always Predicts Winners

@AlphabetSoupchu asks

my friend remembers every card they called correctly. How do I judge whether their advice is actually good?

Jeff answers

I'd ask about the full record, including misses, purchase timing, costs, and whether suggested sales were realistic. Selective examples can make anyone look unusually accurate. Listen to their reasoning, then verify card references in our app yourself. Borrowing an idea doesn't require handing over your judgment.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
