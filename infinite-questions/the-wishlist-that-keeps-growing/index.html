The Wishlist That Keeps Growing

@CharizardOnABudget asks

my wishlist went from 6 cards to about 70. How do you pick what to buy first without just buying whatever catches your eye that day?

Jeff answers

I separate “I'd like that someday” from “this is my next card.” Pick a few favorites, check their current reference prices in the infinitepulls.com app, and set a comfortable limit for each. That gives you something to compare a tempting purchase against. The long list can stay long. Writing a card down doesn't mean you've promised to buy it.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
