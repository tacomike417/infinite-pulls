What is it worth?

The Mystery Box Temptation

@FreshPackFrank asks

mystery box says somebody can get a huge chase card. Looks fun but how do you decide if it's worth trying?

Jeff answers

I look for clear contents, how the chase is distributed, and whether the seller explains what most buyers receive. I use our app to check identifiable cards instead of taking the advertised value at face value. If the ordinary outcome would disappoint me at that price, I pass. I treat the surprise as entertainment spending, never as a dependable way to come out ahead.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
