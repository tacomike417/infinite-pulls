A Gift Card Is Still Money

@NewKidInKanton asks

got store credit as a gift and suddenly everything seems free. How do you avoid blowing it?

Jeff answers

I'd write down the balance and choose a goal before browsing. It's money someone gave you to enjoy, and you can enjoy taking your time with it. Check any applicable terms so you know how it works. You don't need to spend the entire balance in one visit.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
