What is it worth?

Minimum Order Size

@ReprintRookie asks

shipping a single tiny-value card takes forever. Is it reasonable to sell small cards in groups?

Jeff answers

Yes, if the group is clearly described and useful to a buyer. I'd compare card references in our app and account for the packing work before choosing the format. A sensible minimum or themed lot can make small sales manageable. Just explain what buyers receive rather than promising vague “value.”

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
