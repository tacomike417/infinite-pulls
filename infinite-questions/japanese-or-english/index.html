Japanese or English?

@TwoLanguageTrainer asks

found the japanese version way cheaper. Same art, so is that basically a bargain or am I missing something?

Jeff answers

It might be a great way to own art you love. It isn't automatically an underpriced English card, though; different languages have their own supply and buyers. I compare sales in the same language, version, and condition, starting with the correct listing in our app where available. Buy the copy you want to own, and check demand in that language if reselling matters to you.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
