The Gift List That Spoils the Surprise

@BinderMath asks

my family wants a card wishlist but I don't want to turn gifts into a shopping assignment. Any middle ground?

Jeff answers

I'd offer a few exact options at different comfortable prices, plus favorite Pokémon or themes for anyone who enjoys choosing. Include version details so they don't have to decode the hobby. They still get to choose. A little guidance can remove stress without removing the thought behind the gift.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
