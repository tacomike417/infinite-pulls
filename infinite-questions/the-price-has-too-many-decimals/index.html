The Price Has Too Many Decimals

@BinderMath asks

app says a really exact number. Should I price my trade to the cent?

Jeff answers

I'd treat that precision as how the estimate is displayed, not proof of an exact cash value. Our app helps start the conversation; condition and the actual deal still need judgment. For a friendly trade, agree on sensible rounding instead of letting a few cents swallow the evening.
