The Border Color Changed

@OneMorePackPls asks

some cards have yellow edges and some silver. Is one color automatically the good version?

Jeff answers

I'd treat the border as an identification clue, not a ranking. Border styles differ between languages and eras. Compare the complete card with the correct printing before deciding anything about it. You can enjoy either look without assuming one color means a superior collection.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
