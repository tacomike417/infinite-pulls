The Budget Is Averaging Itself Away

@Squintle asks

I say I spend $50 a week but skip one week and spend $200 the next. Is averaging okay?

Jeff answers

I'd use a running total over a period that matches your actual household budget. Skipping one purchase doesn't create unlimited credit for later. Include supplies and delivery costs so the total stays honest. Choose a limit you can comfortably maintain, then check it before the exciting decision.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
