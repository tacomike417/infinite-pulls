The Already-popular Card

@TooManyWeedles asks

is buying something popular automatically safer than buying a weird niche card?

Jeff answers

Popularity can help explain demand, but the purchase price and future supply still matter. I'd use our app to compare the exact card and ask what expectations are already reflected in the price. Familiarity can make a purchase easier to understand without making it immune to losses.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
