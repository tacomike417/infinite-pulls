What is it worth?

The Purchase Record Without a Price

@FreshPackFrank asks

I know I bought a card but can't find what I paid. Do I just use today's value?

Jeff answers

I'd mark the purchase amount unknown until you find a receipt or transaction record. Our app's current reference answers a different question. Don't fill a missing historical cost with a convenient present-day number; that can distort later comparisons. An honest blank is better than false precision.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
