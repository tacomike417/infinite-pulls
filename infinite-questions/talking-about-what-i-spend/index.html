Talking About What I Spend

@SlabConfused asks

I avoid telling my partner what cards cost because I don't want an argument. Thats probably not great?

Jeff answers

I'd move the conversation away from defending one exciting purchase and toward an agreed hobby budget. Be honest about actual spending, including small purchases and supplies. Our app's estimated values don't replace that record. A clear arrangement is easier to live with than hoping collection value justifies a surprise bill.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
