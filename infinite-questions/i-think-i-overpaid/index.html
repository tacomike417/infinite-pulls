I Think I Overpaid

@BuyersRemorsechu asks

paid $80 and now im seeing the same card around $45. Feeling pretty stupid about it tbh

Jeff answers

You're not stupid. First, make sure you're comparing the same version and condition in the infinitepulls.com app; then look at recent sales. If you did overpay, don't rush into another purchase to “make it back.” Figure out whether the price changed or you missed a detail, and use that lesson next time. One purchase doesn't get to decide whether you belong in this hobby.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
