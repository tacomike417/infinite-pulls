Showing a Collection to a Buyer

@FreshPackFrank asks

someone wants to look at my collection. What information should I have ready so we're not there all afternoon?

Jeff answers

I'd prepare a simple list of the main cards, their versions, and honest condition notes. Use the infinitepulls.com app for reference prices; we built it using current market pricing from TCGplayer and Cardmarket. Keep estimates separate from your asking amount. Mention what isn't included before the inspection. A clear starting point leaves more time for useful questions.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
