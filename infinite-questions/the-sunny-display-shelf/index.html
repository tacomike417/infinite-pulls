The Sunny Display Shelf

@FomoFeraligatr asks

my favorite cards look amazing by the window. Is that okay if they're in hard cases?

Jeff answers

I'd choose a display spot away from direct sunlight. A case protects against some physical handling, but I wouldn't assume it blocks damaging light. Enjoy the display where you can see it without giving the cards daily sun exposure. Moving a shelf is an easy improvement.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
