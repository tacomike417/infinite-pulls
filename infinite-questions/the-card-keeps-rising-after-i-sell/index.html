The Card Keeps Rising After I Sell

@SlabConfused asks

sold for a result I was happy with, then it climbed again. How do you not obsess over that?

Jeff answers

I'd remember what information and goals supported the sale when you made it. Check our app if you need a current reference, but don't rewrite the past as though the future was obvious. A satisfactory completed sale doesn't become a mistake every time someone later pays more.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
