The Scan Got the Version Wrong

@ClosetCleanoutCrew asks

a scan found the right pokemon but picked the wrong shiny version. Should I just leave it?

Jeff answers

I'd correct the identification wherever you're recording the card. Recognition can be a starting point, but the exact finish still matters. When pricing it in our app, make sure the selected reference is the one you own. A convenient match isn't helpful if it's the wrong printing.
