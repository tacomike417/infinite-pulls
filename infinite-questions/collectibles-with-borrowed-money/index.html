Collectibles with Borrowed Money

@Squintle asks

everyone's buying a release and I could put it on money I don't really have yet. Bad idea?

Jeff answers

I'd wait until the purchase fits money you can comfortably spare. A collectible doesn't promise a return or an easy sale when a payment comes due. Missing one buying opportunity is easier than making the hobby responsible for solving a debt problem. The next purchase can wait for your budget.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
