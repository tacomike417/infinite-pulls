The Return Date Keeps Moving

@SlabConfused asks

my grading order is taking longer than I expected. Should I start worrying?

Jeff answers

I'd check the company's order status and current service terms, then contact support if the order needs clarification. Keep your submission and shipping records handy. I wouldn't make a sale promise based on an estimated return date. Waiting is frustrating without adding a second deadline you can't control.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
