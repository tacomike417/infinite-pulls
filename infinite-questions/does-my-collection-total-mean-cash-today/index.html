Does My Collection Total Mean Cash Today?

@SpreadsheetSnorlax asks

if my collection says $1,000 does that mean I could sell it for a thousand this weekend? trying to understand the number

Jeff answers

I treat the infinitepulls.com total as an estimate, not a standing cash offer. It depends on matching your cards correctly and checking what condition each price represents. Finding buyers takes time, and fees, shipping, or a bulk discount can reduce what you keep. A thousand dollars spread across many inexpensive cards can take much more work to sell than a few cards with steady demand.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
