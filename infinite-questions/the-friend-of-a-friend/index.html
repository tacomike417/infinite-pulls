The Friend of a Friend

@AccidentalEevee asks

someone in my group says they know the seller. Is that enough of a reference?

Jeff answers

It's a helpful lead, not a replacement for understanding the deal. I'd ask what direct experience they had, then check the item and transaction details myself. Personal connections can make us skip questions we would normally ask. Clear communication protects the friendship as well as the purchase.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
