The Collection Became an Investment After It Dropped

@SealOrNoSeal asks

I bought something for fun, it dropped, and now I say I'm holding it as an investment. sound familiar?

Jeff answers

I'd be honest about the original reason. Calling it an investment doesn't change the card or guarantee recovery. Check the reference in our app, then decide whether you still enjoy owning it and can comfortably keep it. You don't need a more impressive label to explain a hobby purchase.
