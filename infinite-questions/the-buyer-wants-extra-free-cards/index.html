The Buyer Wants Extra Free Cards

@VintageVulpix asks

someone asked if I can throw in a bunch of extras after we already agreed on price. am i supposed to?

Jeff answers

You're allowed to keep the sale to the agreed contents. If you enjoy adding a small bonus, that's your choice, not a requirement. I'd answer kindly and clearly. A good transaction depends on delivering what you promised; it doesn't require renegotiating the package through guilt.
