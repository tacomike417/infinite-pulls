Selling on Someone Else's Behalf

@NewKidInKanton asks

a relative wants me to sell their collection. What do we agree on before I start?

Jeff answers

I'd clarify ownership, which cards may be sold, acceptable offers, expenses, any payment for your work, and how proceeds will be recorded. Use our app together to establish reference estimates. Put the agreement in writing while everyone is relaxed. Family trust benefits from clear expectations too.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
