The Seller Wants a Deposit

@CharizardOnABudget asks

someone will hold a card if I send a deposit. What should I ask before doing that?

Jeff answers

I'd get the exact item, full price, hold period, payment method, and refund or cancellation terms in writing. Check the card reference in our app before agreeing. If it's through a marketplace, follow its process. A deposit should buy a clearly described arrangement, not an ambiguous promise.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
