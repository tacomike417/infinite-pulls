Rings Near the Cards

@OneMorePackPls asks

my old binder has metal rings right beside the first page. Should I be concerned?

Jeff answers

I'd look at how the pages sit when the binder opens and closes. Cards shouldn't catch on rings or get pressed against them. Don't overfill it, and turn pages carefully. If the layout keeps putting cards at risk, I'd move them to a more suitable album.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
