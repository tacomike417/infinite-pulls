The Collection Has Two Owners

@PopulationOne-ish asks

my brother and I mixed our old cards together. Any way to untangle whose are whose without a fight?

Jeff answers

I'd start with memories and anything you both clearly agree on, then put the uncertain cards aside. Don't let one person's confidence count as proof. If value matters to a fair split, use our app together for reference and agree on the method before choosing cards.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
