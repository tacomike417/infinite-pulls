A Card with My Favorite in the Background

@SealOrNoSeal asks

my favorite pokemon is in the background of someone else's card. Does that belong in its collection?

Jeff answers

If that's your collection rule, yes. I'd keep a separate checklist for background appearances because searching only the card name can miss them. You can even make a dedicated page. It's a fun way to look more closely at artwork you might otherwise skip.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
