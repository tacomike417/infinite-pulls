The Card Arrived Better Than Expected

@TradeNightTina asks

is it worth messaging a seller when everything goes right, or do people only bother when stuff goes wrong?

Jeff answers

I'd send a short thank-you and leave an honest review describing what went well, such as accurate condition or careful packaging. Useful feedback helps the next buyer and acknowledges the seller's work. It doesn't need to sound like advertising to be appreciated.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
