The Offer I Declined Was Fair

@SlabConfused asks

I turned down an offer even though the price was reasonable. Is that bad business?

Jeff answers

If you aren't committed to selling, a fair offer doesn't oblige you to accept it. I'd use our app to understand the reference, then separate price from readiness to let go. @JustHereForPikachu's keepsake question applies here. Decide which cards are truly available before inviting more offers.
