The Digital Card Isn't in My Binder

@PokeDadInTraining asks

my game account has a card I love. Does owning that mean I own any physical version too?

Jeff answers

I'd treat the digital collection and the physical collection separately. Don't assume an account item includes a physical card unless a specific product explicitly says so. If you want the artwork in a binder, identify the real printed version independently. Similar-looking collections can operate under very different systems.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
