What is it worth?

A Bundle with “hundreds in Value”

@AlphabetSoupchu asks

someone's selling a whole bundle and says the cards individually add up to way more. Is that automatically a good deal?

Jeff answers

I'd ask for a card list and check the meaningful cards individually in our app. Make sure the total uses the right versions and realistic conditions, and notice how much of it comes from tiny-value cards. A bundle can be convenient, but an impressive sum doesn't guarantee useful inventory or easy sales. I want to know what I'm buying, not just what somebody added up.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
