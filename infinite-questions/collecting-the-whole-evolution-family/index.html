Collecting the Whole Evolution Family

@PopulationOne-ish asks

I started with one favorite and now want its whole evolution line. Is there a sensible way to organize that?

Jeff answers

I'd choose whether each page follows an evolution line within one set or mixes your favorite artwork across sets. Try a sample page before buying to fill every space. A collection rule should help you choose cards, not turn a fun idea into an immediate shopping list.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
