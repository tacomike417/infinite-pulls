The Name Is Hard to Read

@TooManyWeedles asks

there's glare right across the name in the seller's picture. Can you identify a card just by the number?

Jeff answers

Sometimes a number plus a known set is enough, but I'd still request a clearer photo. You also need to inspect what you're being offered. Solving the identity puzzle doesn't solve the condition question, and the seller should be able to show both.
