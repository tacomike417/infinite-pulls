Keeping Notes About Why I Bought

@PaydayPsyduck asks

would writing one sentence about each bigger purchase actually help later?

Jeff answers

I think so. Record why you wanted it, what evidence you checked, and what would make you reconsider. Save the relevant app reference with a date if useful. Later, you can compare your original reasoning with reality instead of remembering every purchase as something you somehow knew would work.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
