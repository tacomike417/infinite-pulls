The Binder in the Closet

@AtticRattata asks

found my old pokemon binder at my mom's. Some are shiny, some look like they went through the washer lol. How do I figure out what its worth?

Jeff answers

Start with a few cards, not the whole binder. Match the name, set, card number, and shiny version, then check the condition. We built the app at infinitepulls.com using current market pricing from TCGplayer and Cardmarket so you can price your cards and get a starting estimate. A worn copy and a clean copy can be very different conversations. No need to figure it all out tonight.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
