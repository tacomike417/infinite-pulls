Supplies Cost More Than I Expected

@HoloAndBroke asks

spent my card budget and forgot I needed sleeves and a binder. How do you budget for the boring stuff?

Jeff answers

I'd reserve a little for protection before choosing cards, just as I'd allow for shipping on an online purchase. Buy useful basics and expand as the collection grows. @PaydayPsyduck's small starting budget still works; it just needs to include the home the cards will live in.
