The Person Who Never Brought a Binder

@PaydayPsyduck asks

friend comes to trade night but just watches. Should we push them to bring something next time?

Jeff answers

I'd invite them without making participation depend on trading. They might be learning, saving, or simply enjoying the company. Ask whether they'd like help putting together a small trade selection, then let them decide. Watching a few good conversations can teach a beginner a lot.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
