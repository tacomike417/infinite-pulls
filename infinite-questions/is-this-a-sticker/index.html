Is This a Sticker?

@TradeNightTina asks

found something pokemon-shaped in the binder but it's thinner and the back looks peelable. Is that a card?

Jeff answers

It may be a sticker or another collectible rather than a trading card. I'd photograph both sides and identify the product before peeling or testing anything. Store it separately for now. Being outside the card game doesn't make it uninteresting; it just changes which reference you need.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
