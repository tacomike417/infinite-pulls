The Package Says Delivered

@PokeDadInTraining asks

tracking says delivered but the buyer says nothing arrived. Does that mean somebody's lying?

Jeff answers

Not necessarily. I'd keep communication factual and follow the marketplace's delivery-dispute process, including any appropriate carrier inquiry. Save the tracking and shipment records. Don't assume a scan explains everything about where the package ended up, or promise a resolution outside the applicable process before checking.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
