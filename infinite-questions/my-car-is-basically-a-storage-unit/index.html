My Car Is Basically a Storage Unit

@SlowpokeSavings asks

I keep my trade binder in the car so I always have it. Bad habit?

Jeff answers

I'd bring it indoors when you get home. Cars can experience large temperature swings, and a visible binder can attract unwanted attention. Keep a small reminder with your keys if you need one. Being ready to trade shouldn't require leaving your collection unattended all week.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
