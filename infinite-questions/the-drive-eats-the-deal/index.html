someone will pay a little more if I drive across town. How do you decide if it's worth it?

Jeff answers

I'd compare what you keep after travel time and costs with the simpler option, using our app for the card reference. @CharizardOnABudget asked about the gap between sticker-price profit and actual proceeds. You can decline an inconvenient arrangement even if its headline offer is slightly higher.
