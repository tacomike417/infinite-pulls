No Price Doesn't Mean No Value

@ChecklistChikorita asks

can't find a price for one of my cards in the app. Does that mean its worthless or did I win the lottery

Jeff answers

Neither conclusion follows from a missing price. It might be an unusual printing, a mismatched listing, or simply limited data. I start by checking the set, number, language, and finish in the infinitepulls.com app. If it still isn't there, I look for exact comparable sales or ask someone familiar with that card. An empty field means we need more information.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
