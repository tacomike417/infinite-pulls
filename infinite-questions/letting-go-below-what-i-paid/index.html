Letting Go Below What I Paid

@BinderMath asks

I want to sell something I don't enjoy but keep waiting to get my original money back. What would you consider?

Jeff answers

I'd check the current reference in our app and compare keeping it with a realistic sale now, including costs. Your purchase price is useful history, but it doesn't set today's buyer demand. @BuyersRemorsechu asked about overpaying; keeping an unwanted card forever doesn't necessarily repair that purchase.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
