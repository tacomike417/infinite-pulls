Inspecting Before Local Payment

@PaydayPsyduck asks

meeting someone for a card. Is it awkward to check it properly before handing over money?

Jeff answers

I'd agree in advance that you'll inspect the actual card before completing the purchase. Use an appropriate, well-lit public meeting place and allow enough time. Bring the reference details you need. A calm inspection is a normal part of buying an item whose identity and condition matter.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
