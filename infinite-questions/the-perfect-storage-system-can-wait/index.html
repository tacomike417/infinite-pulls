The Perfect Storage System Can Wait

@AccidentalEevee asks

I keep researching binders instead of sorting anything. How do I stop making this such a project?

Jeff answers

I'd choose a sensible temporary setup and organize one manageable batch today. Write down what actually bothers you about that setup before buying an upgrade. Real use will teach you more than endless comparisons. Safe, understandable storage now is better than a perfect plan beside loose cards.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
