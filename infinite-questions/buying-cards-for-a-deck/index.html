Buying Cards for a Deck

@TooManyWeedles asks

I found a deck list online. Can I just buy those cards and enter an event?

Jeff answers

I'd confirm the event's format and current legality rules with the organizer first, then check that the list matches them. Price the exact cards in our app once you've verified what you need. An old or different-format deck list can be useful inspiration without being ready for your event.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
