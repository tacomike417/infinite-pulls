The Adult Who Feels Too Old

@JustHereForPikachu asks

I want to start collecting but feel weird being older than most people I see posting. Too late?

Jeff answers

Not at all. Start with what interests you now and ask questions at your own pace. @NewKidInKanton was still learning after several months; being new is a stage, not an age limit. You don't need childhood nostalgia or a competitive collection to enjoy the cards in front of you.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
