The Anniversary Argument

@CornerConcerned asks

someone says an anniversary guarantees cards will go up. Is that a real collecting rule?

Jeff answers

I'd treat it as a claim to investigate, not a rule. Attention can change without producing the price someone predicts, and expectations may already be reflected in asking prices. Use our app for current references. Buy with an explanation stronger than the calendar alone.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
