What if I Need the Money Back?

@NervousNinetales asks

I've been buying cards thinking I could just sell them if I needed cash. Starting to realize that might not be instant

Jeff answers

That's a useful realization to have before you're under pressure. Our app can help estimate market value, but it can't promise a buyer or a deadline. I keep money I'll need soon separate from card purchases, because a quick sale may mean accepting less. If you already need the cash, start comparing realistic offers early rather than waiting for an ideal price.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
