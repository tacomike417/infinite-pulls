Helping Family Understand the Collection

@CharizardOnABudget asks

if I couldn't explain my collection for a while, my family would have no clue what any of it is. What notes help?

Jeff answers

I'd leave a plain inventory, where the records are kept, and contact details for someone you trust to help identify cards. Explain that our app offers market references, not guaranteed cash offers. Keep the notes accessible to the right person and avoid exposing account passwords in an ordinary shared document.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
