A Collection with a Theme

@NewKidInKanton asks

I like the cards where pokemon are just doing regular stuff. Sleeping, eating, hanging out. Is that a collection or just me being weird lol

Jeff answers

That's absolutely a collection. A theme you genuinely enjoy gives you a reason to choose one card over another, which makes browsing more fun and spending more intentional. I'd start a list of those everyday scenes and see what connects them. You don't need a complete set or an expensive centerpiece for your binder to tell a story.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
