When New Information Changes the Plan

@FreshPackFrank asks

I learned something that makes me less confident in a purchase. Is changing my mind just being emotional?

Jeff answers

Not necessarily. I'd write down the new information and whether it affects your original reason for buying. Review the relevant reference in our app, then decide calmly. Sticking to a plan isn't useful if it requires ignoring evidence. The goal is a reasoned decision, not proving you never reconsider.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
