The Wrong Copy Ended Up in the Trade

@VintageVulpix asks

I meant to trade my duplicate but accidentally handed over the nicer copy. Can I ask to swap them?

Jeff answers

I'd contact the other person promptly and explain exactly what happened. Ask rather than assume they're obligated to change a completed trade. This is why I separate available copies before an event. If they agree, confirm which cards are being exchanged back so the correction stays clear.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
