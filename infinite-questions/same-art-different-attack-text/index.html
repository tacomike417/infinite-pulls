Same Art, Different Attack Text

@ChecklistChikorita asks

two cards share art but the words aren't exactly the same. Is that a fake giveaway?

Jeff answers

I'd compare each with its own exact release before deciding. Reprints, languages, and different products can complicate a side-by-side comparison. A mismatch is worth investigating, but it isn't a complete diagnosis. Keep the photos and ask someone familiar with those specific printings.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
