@TwoLanguageTrainer asks

I'd choose a short list and define the condition improvement that would actually satisfy you. Check references in our app and set spending limits before browsing. Once a replacement meets the goal, stop moving the goalposts. @CornerConcerned's tiny flaw question is a useful reminder to decide what truly matters.
