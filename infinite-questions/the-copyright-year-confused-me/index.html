The Copyright Year Confused Me

@HoloAndBroke asks

the little date doesn't match the year someone told me this card released. Which one do I believe?

Jeff answers

I wouldn't identify a printing from the copyright line alone. I'd compare the full card with a reliable checklist for that specific product. Copyright text and product-release information aren't interchangeable. Keep both details in your notes until you've confirmed which printing you're holding.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
