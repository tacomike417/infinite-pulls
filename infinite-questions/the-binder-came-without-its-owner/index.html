The Binder Came Without Its Owner

@NewKidInKanton asks

My uncle gave me a binder but knows nothing about it. Where do I start when I don't even recognize half the cards?

Jeff answers

Oh, this reminds me of @AtticRattata finding that old binder. I'd photograph each page before rearranging anything, then identify a few cards at a time. Keep any notes or packaging that came with it. Learning what you have comes before deciding what to keep or sell.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
