The Regular Version Is Prettier to Me

@TwoLanguageTrainer asks

I actually like the cheap regular version better than the fancy one everyone wants. Am I missing the point of collecting?

Jeff answers

I think you've found your point. If the regular version is the one you enjoy looking at, you don't need to upgrade it to satisfy somebody else's checklist. I like having a reason for a purchase that survives the group chat losing interest. Choose the artwork you want to live with. An expensive version isn't automatically a better fit for your binder.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
