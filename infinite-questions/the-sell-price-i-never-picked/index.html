@FomoFeraligatr asks

I bought a card to eventually sell but never decided what eventually means. How do you plan that?

Jeff answers

I'd write down why you bought it, what result would justify selling after costs, and what would make you reconsider sooner. Check our app for a market reference when reviewing it. The plan can change with new information, but having one helps separate a decision from a mood.
