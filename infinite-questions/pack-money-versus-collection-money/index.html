Pack Money Versus Collection Money

@KitchenTableTrainer asks

should I have separate budgets for packs and cards I deliberately want?

Jeff answers

I find that distinction useful. Packs buy the experience of opening; individual cards buy a known item. Oh, this is where @OneMorePackPls ended up while chasing that Gengar. Separate limits let you enjoy both without quietly using your planned-card money to keep a pack session going.
