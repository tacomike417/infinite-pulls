Shipping Changes the Comparison

@ReprintRookie asks

one card is $8 with $6 shipping and another is $12 shipped. Which number do you use?

Jeff answers

For deciding what to buy, I'd compare the total delivered cost. Our app provides a card-price reference; shipping is a separate part of your actual purchase. That distinction matters most on inexpensive cards, where postage can be a large share of the bill.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
