The Floor Seemed Convenient

@PaydayPsyduck asks

quick question, is keeping card boxes on the basement floor a bad idea even if the boxes have lids?

Jeff answers

I'd raise them off the floor and choose a dry, stable location. A lid doesn't make an ordinary card box waterproof. Remember @KitchenTableTrainer's sandwich bag? Better storage means thinking about the surroundings as well as the container. Think about where water could reach before it does.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
