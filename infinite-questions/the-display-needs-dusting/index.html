The Display Needs Dusting

@CornerConcerned asks

how do I clean around displayed cards without getting cleaner on them?

Jeff answers

I'd remove the protected cards first, clean the empty display area, and let it dry completely before putting them back. Spray onto an appropriate cleaning cloth away from the collection. It's a small extra step that avoids turning routine dusting into an experiment with card surfaces.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
