The Unopened Gift I Want to Save

@ReprintRookie asks

got sealed product as a gift and actually want to keep it sealed. Is that disappointing for the person who gave it?

Jeff answers

I'd explain that preserving it is one way you enjoy the gift. You could show them where it will go in your collection or talk about why that product matters to you. @SealOrNoSeal wondered whether to open a birthday box. The decision can come from enjoyment, not a predicted fortune.
