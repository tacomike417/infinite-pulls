The Personal Binder at Trade Night

@KitchenTableTrainer asks

can I bring a binder just to show people even if none of it is for trade?

Jeff answers

Yes, but I'd say that up front and keep it separate from your trade binder. “This one's just for looking; this other one is available” prevents disappointment. People can enjoy your collection without negotiating for it. Clear labeling makes the invitation easier for everyone.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
