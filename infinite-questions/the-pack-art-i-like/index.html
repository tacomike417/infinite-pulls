The Pack Art I Like

@PokeDadInTraining asks

can I collect sealed packs because of the pictures on the wrappers, or is that missing the point?

Jeff answers

You can collect the packaging itself. I'd define whether you want individual artworks, complete artwork groups, or favorite eras, and protect the packs without squeezing them. Make sure sealed authenticity is part of your buying process. The reason you enjoy an item doesn't have to match the next collector's reason.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
