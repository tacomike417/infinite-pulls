Bringing a More Experienced Friend

@SealOrNoSeal asks

is bringing a buddy who knows cards overkill for my first bigger purchase?

Jeff answers

I think it's sensible if they help you understand rather than take over. Tell them your budget and what you're unsure about beforehand. You should still be able to explain why you're buying the card. The best help leaves you more informed for the next purchase too.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
