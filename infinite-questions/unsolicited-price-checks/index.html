every time I show a favorite someone announces what it's worth. How do I steer that somewhere else?

Jeff answers

I'd say, “I'm keeping this one; I really wanted to show you the artwork.” @JustHereForPikachu's first pull was about the memory, too. You can appreciate market knowledge without making every conversation a valuation. Ask the other person what they like about the card and change the focus.
