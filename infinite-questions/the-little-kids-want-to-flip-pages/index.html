The Little Kids Want to Flip Pages

@BuyersRemorsechu asks

family keeps wanting to look through my good binder. How do I let them enjoy it without hovering?

Jeff answers

I'd sit with them, put the binder flat, and show how to turn a page without tugging at pockets. A separate hands-on binder can work well for younger visitors. Make the invitation clear: looking together is welcome, pulling cards out needs permission. Simple expectations beat a nervous surprise reaction.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
