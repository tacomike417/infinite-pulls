A Crack in the Holder

@TwoLanguageTrainer asks

found a slab with a crack in the corner. Seller says the card is fine and its just cosmetic. Would that bother you?

Jeff answers

I'd want a close look before deciding. A cracked holder can raise questions about protection, damage, or tampering, even if the card appears fine in photos. I would ask the grading company about its reholder process rather than assume the original assessment settles everything. If I couldn't get comfortable with the condition of the holder, I'd pass. There will be another card.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
