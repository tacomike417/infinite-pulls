Canceling Before Shipment

@CornerConcerned asks

buyer changed their mind right after paying. Is canceling always a bad thing?

Jeff answers

I'd check the order status and the platform's cancellation procedure, then respond promptly. A workable cancellation before shipping can be simpler than a dispute later, but the applicable terms still matter. Keep any refund inside the approved process so both sides have a clear record.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
