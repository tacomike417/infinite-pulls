But It Came Straight from a Pack

@FreshPackFrank asks

how can a brand new card NOT be a 10? I literally put it straight into a sleeve

Jeff answers

I understand why that feels backward. A card can leave the factory with uneven borders, print lines, or imperfect edges. A sleeve protects the condition it already has; it doesn't change what happened before you opened it. Check the front and back under good light, but don't feel you have to become a grader overnight. Fresh from a pack is a starting point, not a promised grade.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
