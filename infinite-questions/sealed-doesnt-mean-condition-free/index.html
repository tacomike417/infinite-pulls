Sealed Doesn't Mean Condition-free

@JustHereForPikachu asks

does condition matter for sealed boxes if nobody's buying them to admire the cardboard?

Jeff answers

I'd inspect the packaging, wrap, seals, and any crushing or damage. Sealed collectors may care about presentation and confidence in the item as sold. Don't assume “unopened” answers every condition question. Clear photos and an accurate description matter for packaged products too.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
