Should I Keep Resubmitting?

@FreshPackFrank asks

if I keep sending a card back, eventually it'll get a better grade right?

Jeff answers

I wouldn't plan around that. Another assessment can cost money without improving the result, and repeated handling adds work and risk. I'd ask what specific evidence supports a review before spending again. Wanting a different number is understandable; it isn't a strategy by itself.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
