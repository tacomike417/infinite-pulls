Playing with a Favorite

@BuyersRemorsechu asks

I'd love to play using a favorite card but I'm nervous about wear. What would you do?

Jeff answers

I'd decide whether that exact copy needs to be the playing copy. Appropriate sleeves and careful handling help, but regular play still involves handling. If a suitable less-pristine or alternate legal printing works, you might keep the sentimental copy protected. Ask a knowledgeable player about valid alternatives.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
