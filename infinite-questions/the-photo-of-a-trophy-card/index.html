The Photo of a Trophy Card

@BinderMath asks

somebody showed me a photo of an amazing trophy card and now I want one. Do normal shops carry stuff like that?

Jeff answers

I'd identify exactly what you're seeing first; some unusual releases have very specific distribution histories. Ask a shop whether it has experience with that category. For expensive or unfamiliar items, ordinary card-shopping habits may not provide enough background to judge the offer confidently.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
