The Coupon That Expires Tonight

@AtticRattata asks

got a coupon and now I'm shopping for something to use it on. Thats backwards, isn't it?

Jeff answers

I'd go back to the want list you had before the coupon arrived. If nothing on it fits comfortably, let the coupon expire. A discount has no obligation attached. I'd borrow the approach from @CharizardOnABudget's wishlist question: pick a genuine next purchase before browsing.
