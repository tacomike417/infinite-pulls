The Price Target from a Video

@BuyersRemorsechu asks

a video says a card will hit a specific price next year. How seriously do you take that?

Jeff answers

I'd ask what evidence and assumptions produce the target, and what could make it wrong. Our app gives a current reference, not a future promise. If the argument is mainly enthusiasm, I treat the number as speculation. A precise prediction can still be a guess.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
