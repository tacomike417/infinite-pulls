Selling Doesn't Require Buying Again

@PaydayPsyduck asks

whenever I sell a card, I immediately buy another. Is that just how collections grow?

Jeff answers

It can be a choice, but it doesn't have to be automatic. I'd decide where sale proceeds are going before listing: another card, supplies, savings, or ordinary expenses. Money coming out of the hobby is still allowed to stay out. A sale isn't a shopping assignment.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
