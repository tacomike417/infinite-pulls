The Auction Hasn't Ended

@TooManyWeedles asks

there's an auction at $20 right now. Can I use that as a comp for mine?

Jeff answers

I'd wait for a completed result and check the details. An active bid is an unfinished negotiation, and the final amount may change. Use our app for the current reference while you wait. A listing that's still running tells us something about interest, but not a completed sale price.
