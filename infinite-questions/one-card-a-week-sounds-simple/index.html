One Card a Week Sounds Simple

@ClosetCleanoutCrew asks

thinking of a one-card-a-week goal. is that a good way to control spending?

Jeff answers

It can limit quantity, but cards don't all cost the same. I'd pair any purchase rhythm with a money limit and permission to skip weeks. Use our app when comparing a specific card. The goal should support your collection, not require buying something just because another week passed.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
