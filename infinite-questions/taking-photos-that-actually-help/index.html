Taking Photos That Actually Help

@BinderMath asks

trying to sell one card and every photo either has glare or looks like I took it inside a cave. Any tricks?

Jeff answers

I use bright, indirect light, a plain background, and a clean camera lens. Photograph the full front and back straight on, then take closer angled shots of anything a buyer should notice. Avoid filters that change the colors or hide wear. Before posting, zoom in yourself: if you can't judge the corners and surface, the buyer probably can't either.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
