The Wishlist Cleanout

@Squintle asks

my wishlist has stuff I only wanted because everyone else did. do you delete those or keep them for someday?

Jeff answers

I'd remove or archive anything that no longer interests you. @CharizardOnABudget's long wishlist needed priorities; sometimes the right priority is letting an old want go. A wishlist should reflect your current taste. Keeping an item forever doesn't prove you were right to want it initially.
