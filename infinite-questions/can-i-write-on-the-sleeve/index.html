Can I Write on the Sleeve?

@BinderMath asks

I wanna put purchase dates on sleeves so I remember. Is that okay?

Jeff answers

I'd use a separate label on an outer holder or a written inventory entry. Keep inks, adhesives, and pressure away from the card itself. That also makes it easier to change your notes without repeatedly handling the card. A simple ID number can connect the holder to your record.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
