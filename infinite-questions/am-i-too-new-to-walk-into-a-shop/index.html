Am I Too New to Walk into a Shop?

@JustHereForPikachu asks

honestly I wanna come in but I know like 4 pokemon and dont wanna ask something everybody already knows. Is it weird to just bring a binder and ask for help?

Jeff answers

Not weird at all. Bring the binder and start with one question. You don't need the vocabulary or a valuable collection to have a place here. I'm an ex-Pokédumb; I didn't arrive knowing all this either. If I use a term that doesn't make sense, stop me and we'll unpack it. I'd rather help you understand one card than leave you nodding through a conversation that didn't help. — Jeff, infinitepulls.com EDITORIAL REFERENCE NOTES — NOT PART OF THE COLUMN These are drafted questions from fictional usernames, not actual submitted letters.
