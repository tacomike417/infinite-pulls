Reusing Last Month's Photos

@FomoFeraligatr asks

can I reuse photos from an identical card I sold before if this one looks about the same?

Jeff answers

I'd photograph the actual copy, especially when showing condition. “About the same” can hide the exact corner or surface mark a buyer cares about. If a platform uses representative images, make that clear and describe the offered condition correctly. Never present another copy's detailed photos as this one's evidence.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
