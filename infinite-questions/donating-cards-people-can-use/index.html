Donating Cards People Can Use

@BinderMath asks

I want to give away extra cards. Should I just drop off a random box somewhere?

Jeff answers

I'd ask the recipient organization first about what it wants and accepts. Sort out damaged or questionable items and package the rest so they're easy to handle. If they're for beginner play, a knowledgeable organizer can help identify useful groups. A thoughtful donation solves a need instead of moving your sorting project.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
