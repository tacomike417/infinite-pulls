Leaving with Nothing

@ClosetCleanoutCrew asks

went to a shop and didn't buy anything because nothing really grabbed me. Why do I feel guilty?

Jeff answers

Browsing respectfully doesn't require a purchase. Ask before taking lots of staff time for a complex valuation, but you can look around and leave kindly. I'd rather you find something you genuinely want than buy a card as an apology for walking through the door.
