In Person Versus Online

@PokeDadInTraining asks

a local copy costs a little more than the one online. Am I silly for wanting to inspect it first?

Jeff answers

No. I'd check the reference in our app, compare the online delivered total, and decide what in-person inspection and convenience are worth to you. A modest difference can be a reasonable personal choice. Just know which benefit you're paying for instead of assuming the numbers are identical.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
