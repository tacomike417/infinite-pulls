Still Liking It if It Never Rises

@SlowpokeSavings asks

is “I'd still like owning it” enough of a reason to buy a card?

Jeff answers

For an affordable personal collection, that can be a very good reason. It doesn't turn the purchase into a sound investment by itself. Check our app for a pricing reference, then be honest about which kind of decision you're making. Enjoyment deserves its own place in the budget.
