The Offer Changed Mid-conversation

@ReprintRookie asks

we agreed on a trade, then they checked a new price and wanted more. Nothing's exchanged yet. What now?

Jeff answers

I'd pause and agree on the reference before restarting. Our app can provide a common starting point, but neither person has to accept revised terms. If you're still discussing the deal, you can walk away politely. Confusion about whether it was final is a good reason to make the steps explicit.
