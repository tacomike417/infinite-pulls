I know a lot about a small niche. Is that an advantage when buying to resell?

Jeff answers

Knowledge can help you identify and describe items accurately, but you still need buyers. I'd check our app where it covers the card and investigate relevant completed sales. Be especially realistic about selling time. Expertise is useful information, not a guarantee that your niche is liquid.
