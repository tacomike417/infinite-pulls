What Do I Do with All the Extras?

@TooManyWeedles asks

I have approximately a million commons taking over my dining room. Is bulk worth anything or do I now live in a pokemon warehouse

Jeff answers

Welcome to the storage part of the hobby. I'd sort out playable cards, promos, and anything with collector demand before treating the rest as bulk. Check possible standouts in our app; bulk offers usually work differently from individual card prices. Ask shops what they currently accept before hauling boxes over. Trading, building beginner decks, or donating to a group that actually wants cards can also give the extras a good home.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
