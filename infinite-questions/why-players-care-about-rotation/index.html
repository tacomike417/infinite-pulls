Why Players Care About Rotation

@ChecklistChikorita asks

people say rotation might affect demand. What does that mean for someone who only collects?

Jeff answers

Some organized-play formats change which sets are eligible, so player demand for certain cards can change. I'd check current official rules before drawing conclusions about a particular card. Our app helps with the market reference, but collectible appeal and gameplay demand may move differently. One rule change doesn't explain every price.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
