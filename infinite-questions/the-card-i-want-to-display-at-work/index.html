The Card I Want to Display at Work

@AccidentalEevee asks

I'd like a favorite on my desk but people handle everything here. Any sensible setup?

Jeff answers

I'd use a stable protective display and choose a spot away from sunlight, drinks, and casual handling. Consider whether that exact valuable or sentimental copy needs to be there. A lower-stakes card you enjoy can make the desk personal without adding constant worry to your workday.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
