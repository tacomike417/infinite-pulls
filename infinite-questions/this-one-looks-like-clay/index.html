This One Looks Like Clay

@FomoFeraligatr asks

some artwork looks like little clay figures instead of a drawing. Are those official too?

Jeff answers

They can be; official card artwork uses a range of media and styles. I'd verify the exact card against a reliable reference rather than judging authenticity by whether the art looks unusual. Different-looking art may simply be the reason you remember that card.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
