Three Reasonable Prices

@ClosetCleanoutCrew asks

I found several fair-looking prices. How do I pick one instead of arguing about the average forever?

Jeff answers

I'd start with our app's reference and decide what the number is for: a patient listing, a quick cash sale, or a trade. Those goals can lead to different reasonable choices. Explain the condition and comparison you used. A defensible range can be more honest than one supposedly perfect answer.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
