The Work Hiding Behind a Sale

@CharizardOnABudget asks

I enjoy selling but sorting, photos, and packing take forever 😅. Is that just me being slow?

Jeff answers

Those tasks are real work. I'd time a few ordinary sales and look for one process to simplify, such as a consistent photo setup or packing station. Don't judge your efficiency by a video that skips the preparation. Understanding the workload helps you choose which sales you actually want to take on.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
