The Receipt Says Miscellaneous

@ReprintRookie asks

bought a valuable card and the receipt is super vague. Should I ask for something more specific?

Jeff answers

I'd ask for a record identifying the actual card and the amount paid, with relevant grading details if applicable. Keep the listing or photos too. You aren't being fussy; a clear purchase record helps with your inventory and any later questions about what changed hands.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
