The Goal I Don't Care About Anymore

@AccidentalEevee asks

I started a collection challenge and now I'm just not excited about it. Is quitting it a waste?

Jeff answers

I'd ask which parts you still enjoy and keep those without forcing the original plan. You learned something about your tastes. If selling or trading the rest becomes useful, check references in our app and proceed deliberately. An unfinished challenge doesn't have to become a permanent obligation.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
