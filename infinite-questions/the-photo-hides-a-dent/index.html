The Photo Hides a Dent

@SealOrNoSeal asks

one lighting angle makes a dent invisible. Should I use the prettier picture as the main one?

Jeff answers

You can have a clear overall photo, but I'd also show the dent deliberately and disclose it in writing. Accurate presentation shouldn't depend on the buyer knowing which lighting trick reveals damage. @BinderMath's condition discussions go better when both people can see the same facts.
