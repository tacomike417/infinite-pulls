seller has loads of good reviews but none for cards. How much does that matter?

Jeff answers

Positive history is useful, but I'd also look for evidence they understand card identification, condition, and packaging. Ask a specific question and notice the answer. A seller can be excellent in another category and still need help shipping a delicate collectible correctly.
