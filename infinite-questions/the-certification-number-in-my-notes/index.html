The Certification Number in My Notes

@AtticRattata asks

should i record slab certification numbers or just the card and grade?

Jeff answers

I'd record the company, grade, certification number, and clear images together. That identifies the particular holder more accurately than the card name alone. @VintageVulpix learned a number isn't an authenticity guarantee, but it's still useful recordkeeping. Keep sensitive purchase and location details private when sharing your list.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
