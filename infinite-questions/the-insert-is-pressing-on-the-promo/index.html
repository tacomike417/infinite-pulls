The Insert Is Pressing on the Promo

@CharizardOnABudget asks

promo is still in the original tray but a corner looks wedged. Leave it alone for authenticity?

Jeff answers

I'd prioritize preventing further damage and get help if the removal looks tricky. Original packaging isn't automatically the safest long-term holder. Photograph how it arrived before changing anything. You can document the original presentation without leaving a card under obvious pressure indefinitely.
