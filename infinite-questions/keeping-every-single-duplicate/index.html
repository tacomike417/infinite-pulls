Keeping Every Single Duplicate

@TooManyWeedles asks

I keep thinking every duplicate might be useful someday. At this rate I'm going to need a second house

Jeff answers

I'd give duplicates a job and a space limit. Keep what you need for decks, trades, or a deliberate collection goal, then regularly review the rest. If something looks like a possible standout, check it in our app before moving it along. “Maybe someday” can fill a lot of boxes. A collection that's easier to manage is easier to enjoy.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
