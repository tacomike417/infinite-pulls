Asking a Shop to Hold Something

@SlabConfused asks

is it rude to ask a shop to hold a card until I get there?

Jeff answers

It's fine to ask, and fine for the shop to have limits. I'd give a realistic arrival time and ask what their hold policy requires. If plans change, let them know. A hold works best as a small commitment both sides understand, rather than something left open-ended.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
