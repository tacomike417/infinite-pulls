The Gold Card from a Party Bag

@TwoLanguageTrainer asks

got a gold-looking card in a party favor. How can I tell if it's a real release or a novelty?

Jeff answers

I'd look for an exact official product match, including its material, number, and design. Gold-colored doesn't settle authenticity either way. Keep it separate until identified. Like @Squintle's questionable bundle, the important step is checking the item itself before assuming it belongs in normal trading inventory.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
