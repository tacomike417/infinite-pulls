The Card Already Has Damage

@AlphabetSoupchu asks

if a card is already beat up, is protecting it kind of pointless?

Jeff answers

I'd still protect a card I want to keep. Existing wear doesn't mean further damage is inevitable or harmless. A sleeve and appropriate storage can help preserve what remains, including the memories. @JustHereForPikachu's first pull matters for reasons a condition label can't capture.
