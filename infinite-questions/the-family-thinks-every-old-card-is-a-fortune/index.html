The Family Thinks Every Old Card Is a Fortune

@AlphabetSoupchu asks

relatives keep telling me a inherited binder must be worth a ton. How do I bring expectations back down kindly?

Jeff answers

I'd suggest identifying and checking a few representative cards together in our app before discussing a total. Explain that printing and condition matter, not just age. @AtticRattata's old binder was the same starting point: discovery first, assumptions later. Shared information is gentler than arguing over hopeful guesses.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
