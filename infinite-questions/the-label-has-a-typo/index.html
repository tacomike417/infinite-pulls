The Label Has a Typo

@AlphabetSoupchu asks

my graded card's label seems to name the wrong version. Does the slab make that correct somehow?

Jeff answers

No. I'd compare the card and label with reliable identification details and contact the grading company about its correction process. Keep photos and order records. Don't sell it under a description you believe is wrong just because the mistake appears on a professional label.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
