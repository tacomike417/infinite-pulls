One Big Buy Versus Several Small Ones

@CharizardOnABudget asks

I saved enough for one major card or several smaller favorites. How do you compare that?

Jeff answers

I'd consider enjoyment, concentration, condition, and how each option fits your budget. Use our app to compare the actual card references, then picture owning each option without any price increase. Neither choice is automatically more grown-up. The better fit depends on what you're trying to build.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
