The Return Terms Are Vague

@OneMorePackPls asks

listing says returns are complicated. Should I ask for details before buying or am I being difficult?

Jeff answers

I'd read the platform's applicable terms and ask the seller to clarify any specific statement that seems inconsistent or unclear. Keep the answer with the transaction. Understanding the process before a problem is much easier than negotiating a new one after the card arrives.
