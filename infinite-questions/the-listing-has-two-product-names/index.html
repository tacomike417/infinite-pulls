The Listing Has Two Product Names

@BuyersRemorsechu asks

title says one thing and the description says another. which one should I assume I'll get?

Jeff answers

Neither until the seller clarifies it in writing. I'd ask for the exact product, language, and contents, with actual photos if needed. Don't let an attractive price persuade you to choose the interpretation you prefer. A clear listing should come before a payment, not after.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
