The Shipping Deadline I Picked

@TooManyWeedles asks

I promised next-day shipping and now work is crazy. How do you keep that from becoming a habit?

Jeff answers

I'd advertise a handling time you can reliably meet, not the fastest turnaround on your best day. If an existing order is delayed, communicate promptly through the platform and follow its requirements. Buyers can plan around an honest expectation better than a repeatedly missed optimistic promise.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
