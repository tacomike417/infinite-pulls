@FomoFeraligatr asks

what's one number you'd suggest a beginner keep track of besides collection value?

Jeff answers

I'd start with total money spent, including the small purchases and accessories. Our app can help with estimated card values, but knowing your actual outlay gives you control over the next month. It's a number you can act on directly, even when the market is unpredictable.
