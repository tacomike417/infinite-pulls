The Allowance Purchase

@CornerConcerned asks

a younger collector wants to spend several weeks of saved allowance on one card. How would you help them think it through?

Jeff answers

I'd help compare the exact card in our app, inspect the condition, and talk about what else that saved money could buy. Give them time rather than urgency. Involve their responsible adult as appropriate. The useful lesson is making an informed choice, not automatically approving or rejecting an expensive-looking card.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
