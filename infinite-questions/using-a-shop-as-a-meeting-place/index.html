Using a Shop as a Meeting Place

@SlowpokeSavings asks

can we just meet at a card shop to do a private sale?

Jeff answers

I'd ask the shop first. It's their space, and they may have rules about outside transactions. Don't assume staff will authenticate the item or mediate the deal either. A quick permission check respects the business and helps you choose a meeting plan that works for everyone.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
