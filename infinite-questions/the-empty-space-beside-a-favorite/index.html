The Empty Space Beside a Favorite

@Squintle asks

I keep wanting to fill a blank pocket even when I don't want any particular card. Why does that happen lol

Jeff answers

An empty space can start feeling like unfinished business. I'd try a temporary divider or rearrange the page before purchasing anything. You might discover you needed a better layout, not another card. Let an actual favorite earn that pocket when you find one.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
