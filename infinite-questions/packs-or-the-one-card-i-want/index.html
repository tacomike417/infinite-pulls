Packs or the One Card I Want?

@OneMorePackPls asks

I only want this one gengar but I keep buying packs trying to get it. At what point am I being ridiculous lol

Jeff answers

You're not ridiculous; the chase is fun. But when I want one specific card, I price that card in the infinitepulls.com app before opening more packs. Buying it individually gives me a known cost; packs don't promise I'll ever pull it. I keep pack money in the entertainment budget. Once that money is gone, the chase can wait.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
