The Card Name Has Extra Letters

@KitchenTableTrainer asks

I thought ex and EX were just people typing differently. Are those actually different things?

Jeff answers

Capitalization can matter in Pokémon card names and mechanics, so I keep it exactly as printed. I'd match the set and card number too. Casual messages often blur those labels, but your collection notes and searches will work better when they preserve the actual name.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
