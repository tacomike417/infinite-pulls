The Card Sold in Two Places

@OneMorePackPls asks

I listed the same card in two spots. How do people stop it selling twice?

Jeff answers

I'd keep one accurate inventory record and promptly remove or pause other listings when a sale is committed under the platform's process. If you can't reliably keep them synchronized, use fewer selling channels. Preventing the mix-up is easier than disappointing a buyer afterward.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
