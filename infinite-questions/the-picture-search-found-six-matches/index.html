The Picture Search Found Six Matches

@NewKidInKanton asks

searched the artwork and got six cards that look identical. How do I narrow this down without losing my mind lol?

Jeff answers

I'd move from the picture to the small print: card number, set marking, language, and any stamp. Then check the foil treatment. Artwork can be reused, so a picture match is a clue, not the finish line. A close photo of the bottom edge usually helps.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
