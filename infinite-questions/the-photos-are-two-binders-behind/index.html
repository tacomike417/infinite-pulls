The Photos Are Two Binders Behind

@PopulationOne-ish asks

my collection photos are really out of date. Do I need individual shots of everything?

Jeff answers

I'd start with clear current page photos, then document higher-value or distinctive pieces individually, including backs and identifying details. Use filenames or notes that connect images to your inventory. You can improve coverage gradually. A useful current overview beats postponing everything until you have time for a perfect archive.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
