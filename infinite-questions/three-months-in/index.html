@NewKidInKanton asks

been at this a few months and still feel like a beginner. Everybody else seems to know everything already

Jeff answers

You're probably noticing more questions because you understand more than you did when you started. That's progress. I'd ask whether you can identify your cards better, explain a purchase, and recognize when you need another opinion. Those are useful collector skills. I'm still happy to learn something across the counter. You don't graduate out of asking questions; you just get better at asking them.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
