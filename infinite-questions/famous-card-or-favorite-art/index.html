Famous Card or Favorite Art?

@AlphabetSoupchu asks

I can buy a recognizable card or one I actually like more that nobody talks about. Which would you pick?

Jeff answers

For a personal collection, I'd lean toward the one you want to look at. If resale is part of the decision, compare both in our app and investigate actual demand. Just don't let the possibility of impressing someone substitute for your own reason to own the card.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
