The Partner Who Doesn't Collect

@NewKidInKanton asks

my partner supports the hobby but doesn't understand why I love it. How do I explain without giving a lecture?

Jeff answers

I'd show one card and tell its story: the artwork, the memory, or the person you found it with. Then ask about something they enjoy collecting or choosing carefully. You don't have to teach rarity categories to explain enjoyment. One concrete example is easier to connect with than the entire hobby.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
