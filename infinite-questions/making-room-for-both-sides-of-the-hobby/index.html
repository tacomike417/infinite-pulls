Making Room for Both Sides of the Hobby

@PopulationOne-ish asks

my friend cares about decks and I care about art. What can we actually do together with this hobby?

Jeff answers

Try showing each other why one card matters to you. They can explain what it does in a deck, and you can point out details in the illustration. You might build a small collection around cards you both enjoy. Shared interest doesn't require identical reasons for collecting.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
