The Boxes in Shared Space

@CharizardOnABudget asks

my collection is spreading into common areas and it's annoying everyone. How do I set a fair boundary?

Jeff answers

I'd agree on specific storage space with the people sharing the home and keep overflow from becoming their problem. When that space fills, review the collection before buying more containers. @TooManyWeedles knows extras can take over quickly. A hobby is easier to support when its physical footprint is predictable.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
