The Buyer Sent a Payment Screenshot

@TradeNightTina asks

I got a screenshot saying payment went through but I don't see it in my account. Ship or wait?

Jeff answers

I'd verify payment through the actual platform or payment account using my normal access, not links in the message. A screenshot isn't confirmation that funds reached the transaction. Wait until the applicable process shows it's ready to fulfill. There's no benefit in guessing about payment status.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
