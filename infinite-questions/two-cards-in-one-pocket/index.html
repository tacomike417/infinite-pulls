Two Cards in One Pocket

@Squintle asks

can I put cards back to back in each binder pocket to save room?

Jeff answers

I'd follow the binder's intended capacity and avoid stretching pockets or pressing cards against each other. If you're running out of space, another suitable binder is simpler than overloading this one. I want pages to turn freely and cards to slide in without force.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
