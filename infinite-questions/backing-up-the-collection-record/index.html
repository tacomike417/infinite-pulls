Backing Up the Collection Record

@JustHereForPikachu asks

my whole collection list lives on one device. Should I keep another copy somewhere?

Jeff answers

I would. Keep a readable backup in a separate secure location and make sure you know how to open it. Include the photos and notes that explain your entries, not just a total. Update it after meaningful changes. A record only helps if you can reach it when the original device isn't available.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
