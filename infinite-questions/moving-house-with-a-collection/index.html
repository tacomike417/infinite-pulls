Moving House with a Collection

@PopulationOne-ish asks

were moving and I really don't want my cards packed under the dishes. How would you handle it?

Jeff answers

I'd inventory the important pieces, secure the cards inside their containers, and pack those containers against movement and crushing. Label the box for careful handling without advertising its value. Keep track of it personally when practical. Knowing where it is matters as much as the padding.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
