The Submission Form Feels Like Homework

@PopulationOne-ish asks

I want to send cards for grading but the forms are making me nervous. What's the first step?

Jeff answers

I'd read the chosen company's current submission instructions and practice identifying one card completely before preparing the whole group. Match the card, form entry, and package order carefully. If you need help, ask before paying or shipping. Confusion is much cheaper to resolve while the cards are still on your table.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
