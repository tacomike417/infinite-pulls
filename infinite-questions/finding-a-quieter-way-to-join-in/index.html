Finding a Quieter Way to Join In

@Squintle asks

big trade nights are a lot for me. Is there a less intense way to meet collectors?

Jeff answers

I'd ask a local shop about quieter visiting times or beginner-friendly gatherings, then start with one question or a small trade selection. You don't have to make a full evening of it. @JustHereForPikachu worried about walking into a shop too. A short, comfortable first visit still counts.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
