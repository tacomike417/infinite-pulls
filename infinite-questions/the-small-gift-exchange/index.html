The Small Gift Exchange

@SpreadsheetSnorlax asks

our group wants to exchange cards but our budgets are all different. How do we make it fair?

Jeff answers

I'd agree on a modest spending limit and a simple theme before anyone shops. If value needs checking, use our app as a shared reference. Keep surprise gifts separate from competitive trading. The point is knowing someone thought of you, not discovering who stretched their budget the furthest.
