Mixing Rarities on a Page

@CharizardOnABudget asks

does putting a cheap common beside a fancy card make the page look less impressive?

Jeff answers

I'd look at whether the two cards work together visually or mean something together. Most people notice the artwork before they know the prices. A common can provide the perfect connection between bigger pieces. Your binder isn't required to arrange itself by somebody else's spending hierarchy.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
