Do I Need White Gloves?

@ReprintRookie asks

people wear gloves in videos. Am I being careless using clean hands?

Jeff answers

I focus on clean, dry hands and controlled handling, usually by the edges. Gloves that reduce your grip can make handling awkward. Whatever you use, avoid touching the printed surfaces unnecessarily. The goal is careful contact, not copying the appearance of an expensive-card video.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
