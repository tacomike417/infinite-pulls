My Card Arrived with a Bent Corner

@SlowpokeSavings asks

card came in and there's a bent corner that wasn't in the listing. I don't wanna start drama but I'm bummed

Jeff answers

Take clear photos of the card and packaging, keep everything, and contact the seller through the platform promptly. Explain what arrived and how it differs from the listing, then ask for a resolution under the site's process. I keep the first message factual and give the seller a chance to respond. You can be kind without accepting something materially different from what you ordered.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
