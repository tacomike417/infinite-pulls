The Tiny Flaw I Can't Unsee

@CornerConcerned asks

bought a card I loved and now all I notice is one little edge spot. How do you know when to upgrade versus just chill out?

Jeff answers

I'd give it some time before shopping for a replacement. Decide whether your goal is a particular condition standard or simply owning that artwork. If the spot genuinely matters after the excitement settles, you can plan an upgrade without rushing. Sometimes looking at the whole binder helps. One tiny flaw doesn't have to become the most important thing about a card you wanted.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
