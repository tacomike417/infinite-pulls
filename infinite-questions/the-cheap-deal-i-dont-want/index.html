found a great deal on something I don't collect. Is passing it up wasting an opportunity?

Jeff answers

I'd check the reference in our app only if I'm seriously considering it, then ask what purpose the purchase serves. A discount on something you'll struggle to enjoy or sell isn't automatically useful. You don't have to become the owner of every reasonably priced card you notice.
