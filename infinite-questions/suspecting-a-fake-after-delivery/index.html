Suspecting a Fake After Delivery

@BinderMath asks

I think something I bought might be fake but I'm not totally sure. Do I accuse the seller right away?

Jeff answers

I'd document the specific differences, keep the packaging, and seek a knowledgeable assessment promptly. Contact the seller through the platform with factual questions and follow the applicable dispute process. @Squintle started with “something feels off,” too; suspicion is the start of checking, not the finished conclusion.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
