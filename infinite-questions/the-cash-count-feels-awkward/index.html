The Cash Count Feels Awkward

@FomoFeraligatr asks

I hate counting money in front of people. How do you do that without seeming suspicious?

Jeff answers

I'd count it calmly together before finishing the exchange. Confirm the agreed total, the item, and any change needed. It protects both people from an honest mistake. You can say, “Let's make sure we've got everything right,” without turning a routine check into an accusation.
