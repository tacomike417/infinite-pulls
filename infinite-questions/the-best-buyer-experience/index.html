The Best Buyer Experience

@HoloAndBroke asks

what makes somebody want to buy from the same person again besides getting a deal?

Jeff answers

I'd focus on accurate descriptions, useful photos, clear communication, and careful fulfillment. Be straightforward when you don't know something and fix your own mistakes. Those habits are repeatable even when you can't offer the lowest price. A buyer remembering “that was easy and exactly as described” is meaningful.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
