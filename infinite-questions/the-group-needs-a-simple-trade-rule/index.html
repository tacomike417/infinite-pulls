The Group Needs a Simple Trade Rule

@TradeNightTina asks

starting a little collecting group. What's one trading rule you'd put front and center?

Jeff answers

I'd start with “Ask, explain, and let people say no.” That covers handling, condition, and pressure. When value matters, use a shared reference such as our app and explain differences calmly. A good group should make a beginner more comfortable asking the next question, not quicker to agree.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
