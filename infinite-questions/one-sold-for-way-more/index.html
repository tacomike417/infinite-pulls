One Sold for Way More

@ClosetCleanoutCrew asks

I found five sold listings around the same amount and one that's WAY higher. Can I use the high one to price mine?

Jeff answers

I'd investigate the odd one before relying on it. Was it the same language, condition, version, quantity, and grading status? Start with our app's reference price, then use several genuinely comparable sales. One unusually high result can make an exciting screenshot without representing what most buyers are paying. I want a price I can explain, not just the biggest number I can find.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
