Getting Ready for a Card Show

@TradeNightTina asks

first card show coming up. Besides money I apparently shouldn't spend all at once, what do I bring?

Jeff answers

I'd bring a short want list, a clear spending limit, protective supplies, and a phone with enough battery to check our app when pricing comes up. Keep personal favorites separate from cards you're willing to trade. Walk around before making a big decision, and give yourself time to compare. You don't have to buy something at every table to have a worthwhile day.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
