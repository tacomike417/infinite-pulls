Packs as a Reward

@PopulationOne-ish asks

thinking of using packs as rewards for family chores. Any way to keep expectations reasonable?

Jeff answers

I'd make the reward the pack-opening experience, never a promised valuable pull. Keep the arrangement within a clear budget and offer alternatives if someone doesn't enjoy collecting. Celebrate the effort that earned it. A disappointing pull shouldn't make the person feel their work earned nothing.

— Jeff, infinitepulls.com
