The Artist Collection Got Expensive

@AccidentalEevee asks

following an artist was affordable until I found their famous cards. Do I have to get those to count as a collector?

Jeff answers

No. I'd collect the work you like within your budget and leave the expensive pieces on a someday list. Check those references in our app when you're ready, not as a daily obligation. Appreciating an artist doesn't require buying the most expensive example of their work.
