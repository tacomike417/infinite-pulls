Card Search — Shop This Card Links

Every card's detail page (My Collection / Wish List search) has a "Shop This Card" section with buttons that send the customer to eBay, TCGplayer, and Cardmarket to shop that card elsewhere. Turning this off removes those buttons — customers will only see prices, not a way to leave the app and buy from a competitor.
