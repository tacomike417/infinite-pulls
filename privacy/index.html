Privacy Policy

Infinite Pulls TCG and Hobby · Last updated 18 September 2026

This is a card shop, not an advertising company. We keep what the app needs to work and nothing else, we do not sell your information to anybody, and you can have any of it deleted by asking.

What we keep

Your account

Your email address, a username, and — only if you add them — a profile picture and a short bio. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and stored scrambled; nobody at Infinite Pulls can see yours, including us.

Your collection

The cards you add, their condition or grade, how many you have, your wishlist, the collector goals you pick, and the cards you scan. This is the app's whole purpose — it is your collection, and it is kept so you can come back to it.

Photos you take

If you photograph your own cards, those pictures are stored so they can be shown back to you and, if your profile is public, to other collectors.

Things you post

Comments, hearts, follows, and any photo you share to the feed. These are visible to other people using the app — that is what posting means.

Alerts

If you turn on notifications, your browser gives us an anonymous address to send them to. Turning alerts off removes it.

Shop orders

When something sells through the website, we keep a record of what sold and when, so the shop knows to set it aside or post it.

We never see your card details. Payment happens on a page hosted by Clover, our payment processor. Your name, address, email and card number are collected there and stay there. They are never sent to infinitepulls.com and we could not look them up if we wanted to.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anybody.
  • We do not run advertising trackers or follow you around other websites.
  • We do not store payment details. See above.
  • We do not read your collection to market to you. The prices you see come from a public card catalog, not from watching you.

Who else touches it

Running an app means using other companies for the parts we do not build ourselves. Each of these sees only the slice it needs:

  • Supabase — stores the accounts and the collection data.
  • Cloudflare — stores and delivers the card photos.
  • GitHub Pages — serves the website itself.
  • Clover — takes the payment, and holds the customer details that go with it.
  • TCGdex — the public card catalog the app looks prices up in. It is sent the card being looked at, never who is looking.
  • Facebook and Instagram — used only for the shop to post its own pictures to its own accounts. No customer information is sent to either of them.

We may also have to hand information over if the law requires it.

Public and private

Your profile starts private. A private collection is visible to you and nobody else. If you make your profile public, your username, profile picture, bio, collection and photos become visible to anybody using the app — and you can switch it back to private at any time, which hides all of it again.

What you can do about it

  • Make your profile private — in your account settings, any time.
  • Delete a photo, a card or a comment — in the app, and it goes.
  • Turn off alerts — in your account settings.
  • Delete your account entirely — email us and we will remove your account and everything attached to it.
  • Ask what we hold — email us and we will tell you.

We will not make you jump through hoops for any of these.

Children

Plenty of children collect Pokémon cards, and they are welcome in the shop. The Infinite Pulls app, though, is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, email us and we will delete it and everything on it.

How long we keep things

Your account and collection stay until you ask us to delete them. Order records are kept as long as the shop needs them for its books.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a change is a significant one, we will say so in the app rather than hoping you re-read this page.
